How MDHD works, and how to get help.

What the app does

MDHD shows today’s doses on one timeline: what’s done, what’s next, and what’s left. Tap Take to log a dose, or Later if you need a minute. A month calendar shows how each day went. You can also see the next dose and take it from Apple Watch.

There is no account, no ads, and no subscription. MDHD is a log and a reminder. It does not give medical advice. Follow your doctor’s or pharmacist’s instructions.

FAQ

Does sync need iCloud? Yes. Medications and history stay on the device you use. To keep iPhone and Apple Watch in sync, sign in to iCloud on those devices. Sync uses your own iCloud account. If iCloud is off, the app still works on that device, but the data will not sync.

Do I need an MDHD account? No. There is no sign-in.

Where is my data? On your device, and in your private iCloud if sync is on. See the Privacy Policy.
