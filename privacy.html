MDHD Privacy Support

Privacy Policy

MDHD collects no data.

What MDHD is

MDHD is a medication log and reminder for iPhone and Apple Watch, published by Michael Shih.

What we collect

We do not collect, sell, or share your information. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party software development kits in the app.

The medications, schedules, and history you enter stay on your device. If iCloud is turned on, that information syncs through CloudKit’s private database in your own iCloud account. Only you can see it. The developer cannot read it.

Notifications

Dose reminders are local notifications on your device. They are not sent through our servers. We do not have servers for this app.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date below.

Contact

For a privacy question, open an issue on the MDHD site GitHub repository.
