MDHD

Your meds stay yours.

MDHD collects no data. There is no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

Where your data lives

Your medications, schedule and history are stored on your iPhone and Apple Watch, and synced through your own private iCloud account. We can't see it.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent to a server.

Drug information

What you type when adding a medication is matched against a list built into the app, so it stays on your phone. Tapping Info fetches one public FDA label file, named by a drug code. Nothing you type or track is sent with it, and nothing is kept on our side.

Deleting your data

Delete the app to remove it from your device. To remove the iCloud copy, go to Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Storage.
